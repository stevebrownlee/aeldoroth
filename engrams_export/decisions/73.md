When outbound LLM requests stalled or took longer than Session.declare's 30s GenServer budget, Session handle_call remained blocked indefinitely, causing GenServer calls to exit with :timeout, player UI to display 'referee: timeout', and the GM Flow board to remain stuck on 'THINKING Waiting for player action...'.
