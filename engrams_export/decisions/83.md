Fix: grammar resolves to/at/towards/with/from NAME and NAME about TOPIC through resolve_believed; buy routes to the room's dossier-role service provider (@provider_roles), never salience guess; resolve carries to: in content_core; perception floors addressed arrivals at F4 same-room / F3 cross-hop with +3 salience cap 10; fold keeps words + addressed_tick beliefs at fidelity>=4; slice.recent_speech exposes per-speaker words/addressed/tick and retains wordless addresses; brain heuristic replies to the last addresser with a rotated bare dossier line (render layer is the single quoter); engine+referee narrate render attributed speech (X says to you vs You hear X say); session concatenates own-action text with new receipts. Extends decision 76 (words cross perception boundary) with addressing. Verified by 3-player live repro (Grevik replies to talker, listener gets ambient room, Mara replies to drink buyer) and 423/423 umbrella tests."
