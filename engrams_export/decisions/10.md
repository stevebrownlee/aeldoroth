The Weaver currently has 4 shards, with 3 remaining lost (including Talven's). The campaign will progress through four tiers (Levels 1-16) following the quest structure: Discovery → Investigation → Confrontation → Consequence. Key NPCs include Master Gearheart (gnome Rekindler who wants to study the crystal), Sister Mournblade (paladin who wants to destroy all crystals), Xareth the Collector (unknowing agent of the Weaver), and The Shadow Weaver (former student of the Pillars who survived the Darkening)."
