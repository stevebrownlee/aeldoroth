# Spatial activation: boundaries are first-class and agent-defined. Every bounded place (region, zone, town, building, dungeon, room) AND every agent/group declares its own boundary at arbitrary granularity (a rat pack scoped to a city, another pack to one building). Boundary triggers: presence crossing, signal arrival, scheduled commitment, coarse tick. Activation wakes bound agents into their cadences (gather beliefs, use capabilities, decide); dormant boundaries skip deliberation; catch-up computed lazily on observable wake
